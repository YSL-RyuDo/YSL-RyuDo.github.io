First author & poster presenter · Simulation study

Consumption Pattern Analysis of In-Game Pricing Policies Using Convolutional Neural Networks

A question from Slap Squad became a study of fixed and dynamic pricing. Simulated player consumption patterns were analyzed using a convolutional neural network.

The question came from setting prices in Slap Squad relative to match rewards. The study simulates 400 players over 30 days and repeats the simulation 20 times, comparing fixed prices with demand-driven prices bounded at 70–130% of the base. The reported 96% CNN classification accuracy describes simulated consumption patterns, not optimal-price accuracy or live-service revenue growth.
